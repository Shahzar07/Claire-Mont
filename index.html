FAQ

Common questions

Claremont Cooling provides air conditioning installation, repair and maintenance for homes and businesses, alongside heating, indoor air quality and preventative HVAC servicing.

An air conditioning system absorbs heat from inside the room through the indoor unit and releases it outside through the outdoor unit, much like a fridge. In heating mode the process runs in reverse, absorbing heat from outdoors and releasing it into the room, just like an air source heat pump.

Yes. Modern air conditioning systems can both heat and cool. Units that heat are known as heat pumps, and they are one of the most efficient ways to heat a room.

Modern systems are very economical. For every 1kW of electricity used, an air conditioning system provides approximately 3–4kW of heating or cooling. Exact running costs depend on room size, insulation and energy prices, but as a rough guide a small-capacity unit can cost around 10–15p per hour to run.

No. Modern indoor units are very quiet: the quietest models run as low as 19 dBA in silent mode, and outdoor units typically run at around 46–49 dBA.
